2. Pick the right first offer — not every possible feature.

One of the most important decisions in a Whop setup is what the customer is actually buying first.

A good stack cannot rescue a confusing offer. A clear offer makes every page, ad, and payment decision easier.

Here are the rules Lucas uses for the first version:

01

Clear outcome

The buyer should understand the result, access, product, or service in one sentence.

02

Clean economics

The price, fulfillment cost, refund risk, and ad budget should leave room to learn safely.

03

Allowed category

The offer and creative must fit Whop and Meta requirements. Whop Ads runs built-in compliance checks before launch.

04

One measurable event

Choose the first event that proves intent: deposit, order, subscription, approved payment, or activated membership.

Local / service

Sell the next step

Use a deposit, package, audit, membership, or paid consultation — whatever reduces friction without creating a fulfillment problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Whop Ads cost to start? +

Whop’s Ads documentation says there is no platform, setup, or monthly fee for running ads through the Whop dashboard; you pay the ad spend. You can fund campaigns with a regular credit card, Whop Card, or eligible pending Whop balance. One ad account is available per Whop business, and category/compliance checks apply.

What are Whop’s payment fees? +

Whop currently lists domestic card pricing at 2.7% + $0.30. International cards, currency conversion, payouts, disputes, tax, billing, orchestration, and other optional services can add fees. Review Whop’s current fee page for your exact use case.

Can a local business use Whop? +

Potentially. A local operator can use checkout links or embedded checkout for deposits, packages, memberships, or paid access, then use compliant paid campaigns to send customers to the offer. Fit depends on the category, fulfillment model, and relevant platform policies.

Can ecommerce and SaaS businesses use it? +

Yes, where the offer and category fit. Ecommerce businesses can use one-time checkout, local payment methods, affiliates, and acquisition tools. SaaS teams can use subscriptions, embedded checkout, webhooks, payouts, and connected accounts while keeping the product on their own domain.

Does Lucas guarantee revenue or ad performance? +

No. Lucas helps with fit, architecture, setup, and launch decisions. Revenue and advertising outcomes depend on your offer, market, creative, budget, execution, margins, and many other factors.
